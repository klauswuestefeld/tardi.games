# Battleship

This game is just a substitue for pencil and paper. It does not enforce rules.

It should do nothing automatically. Users will say if the adversary has hit a ship, for example.

They can make mistakes or even cheat. That's part of the fun.

- Players do not take turns explicitly in the UI.
- Players handle turn-taking informally in real life by calling coordinates out loud.
- The defending player says hit or miss in real life.
- The attacking player manually marks the adversary board in the UI.
- Enemy board cells cycle through `unknown`, `hit` (grey for ship), and `miss` (blue for sea).
- The game does not resolve attacks, enforce turn order, or validate results automatically.
